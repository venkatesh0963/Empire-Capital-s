# 👑 Empire Capital's

**Empire Capital's** is a highly detailed, data-driven wealth and business simulation game built with Next.js, React, and Zustand. 

You start with $100,000 in seed capital and a dream to become the world's most powerful billionaire. The game operates on a real-time simulation engine where time passes day by day, and every financial decision you make impacts your net worth, cash flow, credit score, and global reputation.

---

## 🚀 Features & Gameplay Pillars

- **The Command Center (Dashboard):** A comprehensive financial hub tracking Total Net Worth, Cash Reserves, Passive Income, and Expenses with interactive trajectory charts.
- **Startup Lab (Venture Capital):** Found the next unicorn tech company. Hire developers and marketers, launch products, scale revenue, and negotiate massive exit acquisitions.
- **Empire HQ & Traditional Business:** Buy out or start brick-and-mortar businesses (Retail, Services, Manufacturing, Real Estate). Manage C-Suite executives, adjust pricing, and expand your physical headquarters to unlock global perks.
- **Global Markets & Trading:** Put your liquid cash to work in fully simulated, volatile markets including Stocks, Crypto, Commodities, and Government Bonds.
- **Real Estate & Alternative Assets:** Purchase tangible assets to build generational wealth, from suburban homes to commercial skyscrapers and rare collectibles.
- **Luxury & Lifestyle:** Buy hypercars, private jets, and mega-yachts to increase your Reputation, unlocking exclusive business deals and lowering bank interest rates.
- **Banking & Leverage:** Take out corporate loans based on your Credit Score to finance massive acquisitions.
- **Global Rankings & Rivals:** Compete on a live leaderboard against aggressive AI billionaires who are constantly growing their own net worth.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Framework:** [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router)
- **UI Library:** [React](https://reactjs.org/)
- **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)
- **State Management:** [Zustand](https://github.com/pmndrs/zustand) (with persistent local storage)
- **Icons:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/)
- **Charts:** [Recharts](https://recharts.org/)

## 💻 Local Development

To run the game locally on your machine:

### Prerequisites
- Node.js (v18 or higher)
- npm or yarn

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/venkatesh0963/Empire-Capital-s.git
   cd Empire-Capital-s
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

4. **Play the game:**
   Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) with your browser to see the result.

## 🎮 How to Play (Beginner Strategy)

1. **Start Small:** You only have $100k. Don't blow it on luxury items or volatile crypto right out of the gate.
2. **Build Cash Flow:** Go to the Startup Lab, found a small tech company, hire a developer, and launch it. Wait for it to become profitable and start paying yourself dividends.
3. **Diversify:** Once you have steady cash flow, invest in Real Estate for safe rent, or open a traditional Service business in Empire HQ.
4. **Leverage:** When your passive income is high, go to the Bank and take out a loan to buy a massive asset you couldn't afford with cash alone.
5. **Scale:** Upgrade your CEO skills, expand your businesses to Level 2+, and buy out your competitors until you reach the top of the Global Rankings.
